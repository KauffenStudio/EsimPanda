<objective>
Cut the pricing + coupon data layer over from `src/lib/mock-data/` to live Supabase reads, and make the coupon minimum-order threshold currency-aware.

The pre-existing `formatPrice` JPY formatting bug in `rates.ts` is fixed.

Purpose: CHK-06 (checkout charges the real Supabase retail price; no mock IDs accepted) and CHK-07 (currency-aware coupon minimum). This is the heavier of the two Phase 12 plans.

       const COUPON_MIN_BASE_CENTS = 999;

       /**
        * Per-currency coupon minimum order, in that currency's cents.
        * USD/EUR/GBP: flat 999. BRL/JPY/CNY: converted from a €9.99 base
        * via USD cross-rate (999 EUR-cents → USD → target).

         const usdCents = COUPON_MIN_BASE_CENTS / getRate('EUR'); // 999 / 0.92 ≈ 1086
